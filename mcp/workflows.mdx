Once connected, you drive Keupera in plain language. Below are proven recipes for
classic SEO and for **GEO** (Generative Engine Optimization — being cited by AI
answer engines like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews).

<Tip>
  Start a session with *"Check my Keupera connection"* — the assistant calls
  `keupera_me` to confirm it's authorized and which workspace is active.
</Tip>

## SEO: research → content

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find opportunities">
    *"Research keywords around 'cold brew coffee' for the US market and show the
    best by opportunity vs difficulty."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track the winners">
    *"Add the top 10 to tracking and group them under 'Cold Brew'."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft and schedule">
    *"Draft an article targeting my highest-opportunity keyword, write SEO meta,
    and schedule it for next Monday."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Backlinks & outreach

- *"Create a backlink campaign for 'project management software' in the SaaS niche."*
- *"Show the best opportunities from that campaign and draft an outreach email for the top one."*
- *"Mark that opportunity as contacted and add a note."*

<Note>
  Outreach email is **sent** only when you explicitly ask, and may be disabled
  for OAuth-connected apps depending on your workspace settings.
</Note>

## Measure performance

- *"Summarize traffic for the last 30 days and the top 10 landing pages."*
- *"From Search Console, what queries am I ranking for on page 2 that I should target?"*
- *"Which pages lost the most clicks month over month?"*

## GEO / AI visibility

<Steps>
  <Step title="Baseline">
    *"What's my current AI visibility score?"*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Monitor brand mentions">
    *"Create a Brand Radar campaign with the questions my customers ask AI about
    [topic], running weekly, and show where competitors are mentioned but I'm not."*
  </Step>
  <Step title="See what AI engines read">
    *"Which pages are AI bots like GPTBot and ClaudeBot crawling most?"*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the loop">
    *"Turn the prompts where my brand is missing into content briefs."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for good results

- **Be specific** about market, language, and the website if you manage several.
- **Confirm credit spend** — research, AI generation, and campaigns consume
  credits; the assistant should ask before large or bulk runs.
- **Review before publishing or sending** — treat AI drafts and outreach as a
  first pass you approve.
