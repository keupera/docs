Connect Keupera to [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com) to run research and content
workflows in chat. Use the one-click Keupera app, or add a custom connector.

## Option A — Keupera ChatGPT App (one click)

Add the **Keupera ChatGPT App** to render interactive dashboards directly in
chat. It handles authentication for you — just sign in to Keupera when prompted.

## Option B — Custom connector

Available on **ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Business**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable developer mode">
    Go to **Settings → Connectors → Advanced** and turn on **Developer mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In **Connectors → Create**, name it `Keupera` with URL
    `https://mcp.keupera.com/mcp`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Authorize Keupera in the browser, then enable the connector in your chat's
    tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use it

Ask ChatGPT naturally, for example:

- *"Research keywords for 'vegan protein powder' and add the best 10 to tracking."*
- *"How visible is my brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity this month?"*
- *"Draft an article for my top-opportunity keyword and schedule it for Monday."*

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can't add a custom connector">
    Custom connectors require a Plus, Pro, or Business plan and **Developer
    mode** enabled under Settings → Connectors → Advanced.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Tools don't appear in chat">
    After connecting, enable Keupera in the chat's tools/connectors menu. Confirm
    the URL is exactly `https://mcp.keupera.com/mcp`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
