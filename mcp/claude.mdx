---
title: "Claude.ai & Claude Desktop"
description: "Add Keupera as a custom connector in Claude on the web or desktop."
---

Connect Keupera to Claude on [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) or the Claude
Desktop app as a **custom connector**. You authorize with your Keupera account
in the browser — no API key needed.

## Add the connector

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open connector settings">
    In Claude, go to **Settings → Connectors** and click **Add custom
    connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Keupera server">
    - **Name:** `Keupera`
    - **URL:** `https://mcp.keupera.com/mcp`

    Then click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and authorize">
    Click **Connect**. A browser window opens to sign in to Keupera and an
    **Authorize access** screen — click **Allow**. Claude returns to the
    connector, now showing the Keupera tools.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Custom connectors call your MCP server from Anthropic's cloud, so the
  connection works the same on web and desktop. Your access is stored securely
  by Claude and refreshed automatically.
</Note>

## Use it

Ask Claude naturally, for example:

- *"Research keywords for 'vegan protein powder' and add the best 10 to tracking."*
- *"How visible is my brand in ChatGPT and Perplexity this month?"*
- *"Draft an article for my top-opportunity keyword and schedule it for Monday."*

## Manage access

- **Disconnect:** Settings → Connectors → Keupera → remove.
- **Revoke from Keupera:** **Account → Developer → Connected apps** in the dashboard.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The tools don't appear after connecting">
    Re-open Settings → Connectors and confirm Keupera shows as connected. If not,
    remove it and add it again, making sure the URL is exactly
    `https://mcp.keupera.com/mcp`.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authorization window closes without connecting">
    Make sure you completed the Keupera sign-in and clicked **Allow** on the
    consent screen. Pop-up blockers can interrupt the flow — allow pop-ups for
    claude.ai and retry.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
